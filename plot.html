The Story

On October 1, 1989, forty-three women around the world give birth at exactly the same time, despite none of them having been pregnant when the day began. Intrigued by this extraordinary event, eccentric billionaire and scientist Sir Reginald Hargreeves sets out to adopt as many of the children as possible. He manages to adopt seven of them, believing they possess extraordinary abilities that could be developed and used to save the world.

Reginald raises the children at his mansion, training them to become a superhero team known as The Umbrella Academy. Rather than treating them as ordinary children, he assigns them numbers and pushes them to fulfil his expectations. As they grow older, their different personalities, rivalries and complicated relationships begin to drive them apart. Eventually, the academy falls apart, leaving the siblings to live separate lives.

Years later, the siblings reunite following the mysterious death of their father. Luther, Diego, Allison, Klaus, Five, Ben and Viktor must face the unresolved conflicts of their childhood while attempting to uncover the truth behind Reginald's death. Their reunion is far from peaceful, as old resentments resurface and secrets threaten to tear the family apart once again.

However, the Hargreeves siblings soon discover that their father's death is only the beginning of something much bigger. With extraordinary powers, a deeply dysfunctional family dynamic and a past filled with unanswered questions, they find themselves drawn into events that could change the fate of the entire world.

The Apocalypse

The apocalypse becomes the central threat facing the Hargreeves family when Number Five, who disappeared years earlier, unexpectedly returns. Although he appears to be a teenager, Five has spent decades trapped in a desolate future where humanity has been destroyed. After learning how to travel through time, he returns to the past with a warning: the world is going to end, and his family has only a limited amount of time to stop it.

Unfortunately, preventing the apocalypse proves far more complicated than simply identifying its cause. The siblings must investigate a series of mysterious events while dealing with their personal problems, strained relationships and the consequences of their own actions. As they search for answers, they uncover disturbing truths about their family and discover that the events leading to the end of the world may be connected to people much closer to them than they ever imagined.

With time running out, the Hargreeves siblings are forced to confront their past mistakes and work together despite years of resentment and mistrust. Every decision carries consequences, and attempts to change the future can create new dangers of their own. Their powers may make them extraordinary, but their inability to communicate and cooperate often threatens to undo their efforts.

Ultimately, The Umbrella Academy explores whether a family as fractured as the Hargreeves can overcome its differences and prevent the destruction of humanity. Between time travel, unexpected betrayals, complicated family secrets and increasingly dangerous threats, saving the world becomes a race against time in which the greatest challenge may be learning to trust one another.

Timeline

Powers

The seven Hargreeves siblings, adopted by the mysterious Sir Reginald Hargreeves, each possess a unique supernatural ability that sets them apart. From superhuman strength and teleportation to manipulating energy and communicating with the dead, their powers make them extraordinary.

Number One — Luther

Luther possesses extraordinary physical strength and enhanced durability, allowing him to withstand powerful impacts.

Number Two — Diego

Diego can alter the path of moving objects, making him exceptionally skilled at throwing knives and other projectiles.

Number Three — Allison

Allison can influence people's behaviour by speaking the phrase, “I heard a rumour,” followed by a command.

Number Four — Klaus

Klaus can communicate with the dead, see spirits and, as his abilities develop, allow the deceased to interact with the living world.

Number Five — Five

Five can teleport across distances and travel through time, although controlling these abilities can be difficult.

Number Six — Ben

Ben can summon terrifying tentacles from a portal in his torso, using them to attack and restrain opponents.

Number Seven — Viktor

Viktor can absorb and manipulate sound energy, converting it into powerful destructive blasts and shockwaves.

Trivia

  • The series is based on a comic book created by Gerard Way, the lead singer of My Chemical Romance, and illustrated by Gabriel Bá.
  • Several cast members have musical backgrounds, including Emmy Raver-Lampman (Allison), who performed in the Broadway production of Hamilton.
  • Unlike his siblings, Number Five never receives a conventional first name and is simply known as Five.
  • The umbrella represents the siblings' shared childhood and their complicated connection to the family their father created..
  • The show is famous for pairing unexpected songs with unusual scenes, including a memorable dance sequence in the very first episode. It became one of the series' signature moments.
